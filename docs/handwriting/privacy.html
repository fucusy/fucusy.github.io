Privacy Policy

Updated November 15, 2026

Your handwriting belongs to you. Recognition and search run on your device, and the developer does not receive your diary archive.

This policy covers 纸迹可寻 (Chinese Handwriting Collection), developed by Qiang Chen (app identifier ai.hula.HandwritingCollection). It covers the iPhone and iPad app and explains the separate website and support services below.

What the app stores

Scanned or imported page images, original PDFs, transcriptions, entry dates, corrections, and settings are stored in the app’s local storage. Chinese text recognition and search are processed on the device. No app account is required. The app has no advertising, tracking, developer analytics SDK, or hosted AI service. We do not sell diary information or use it to train models.

Optional iCloud backups

If you turn on automatic iCloud Backup or choose Back Up Now, the app writes ZIP snapshots to your own iCloud Drive. Apple operates iCloud under your Apple account and its privacy policy; the developer cannot access those backups. The app keeps the five newest snapshots it manages. Disabling automatic backup stops future automatic snapshots but does not delete existing copies. This is backup, not live synchronization between devices. Your device’s own Apple backup settings may also include app data, separately from this feature.

Permissions

  • Camera: scan or photograph pages when you request it.
  • Photos and Files: import the images or documents you select using system pickers.
  • Notifications: optional local updates about recognition jobs. The app does not send diary content to a developer push-notification service.

You can manage camera, photo, notification, and iCloud permissions in iOS Settings. Denying camera access still allows file or photo import.

Sharing, exports, and external AI

Copying text, sharing an entry, or exporting a ZIP happens when you choose it. Recipients and storage providers handle the copy under their own policies. If you paste text into an external AI app, that service receives it; this app does not send it to an external AI automatically. ZIP archives are not encrypted by this app. Protect exported files and choose their destination carefully.

Your choices and deletion

  • Edit or delete entries in the collection. A shared original PDF remains while other entries still refer to it; deleting one excerpt does not redact the original PDF.
  • To remove all content from an imported PDF, delete every linked entry, then remove any backups and exported copies containing that PDF.
  • Use Settings → Export & Import to obtain a portable copy of your archive.
  • Disable automatic backup in Settings → iCloud Backup. Delete saved backups there or in Files → iCloud Drive → Chinese Handwriting → Backups. Files may retain deleted items in Recently Deleted.
  • Deleting an entry or uninstalling the app does not remove older iCloud snapshots, device backups, copies saved elsewhere, or copies sent to other services. Manage those separately with Apple or the recipient.
  • To remove all local app data, use iOS Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage → the app → Delete App. Offloading the app retains its data. Local temporary exports and diagnostic files can remain until cleaned by the app/system or the app is deleted.

The developer has no copy of your archive to retrieve or erase remotely. There is no app account to close.

Model files and diagnostics

The recognition model is bundled in the app. Builds 1 and 2 contain a fallback that can request public model files from Hugging Face if bundled files cannot be found; those requests do not upload diary pages, but the download provider receives ordinary network information such as an IP address. Starting with build 3, this fallback is removed: missing bundled assets produce an error instead of a model download. The app does not upload its local diagnostic logs to the developer. Apple may provide TestFlight feedback and crash diagnostics under its own settings and policies.

Website and support

These pages use no advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages and Cloudflare serve the website and may process normal web-request information, including IP addresses, for delivery and security. Visiting this website does not upload your diary. If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request; do not send private diary pages unless necessary. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Updates and contact

We update the date on this page when the policy changes. For privacy questions or requests, contact Qiang Chen at qiang@hula.ai.
