App: Eat Next (iPhone) · Contact: qiang@hula.ai

Eat Next helps you find places to eat nearby, keep track of where you want to go and where you've been, and ranks places by your taste. No account or sign-in is needed, and your data stays on your iPhone.

FAQ

Why does Eat Next ask for my location?

Location is used only while you use the app, to search for places near you with Apple Maps and to show Look Around. It is not stored as a history or sent to us. If you declined, you can turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Eat Next and choose While Using the App.

How does the ranking work?

Eat Next learns from what you do in the app, such as the places you tag and save to lists, and moves places that match your taste toward the top. The ranker runs entirely on your iPhone, so the more you use the app, the better it gets for you, without sending anything anywhere.

How do I export, import or wipe my data?

Open Settings in the app. Export saves your tags, lists and history to a file you can keep or move to another iPhone, Import loads a file you exported earlier, and Wipe deletes all of the app's data from your device.

How do I share a list?

Open a list and tap Share to send it as a link or as an image through any app you like. Nothing is shared unless you send it.
