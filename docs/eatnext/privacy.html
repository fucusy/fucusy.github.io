Eat Next

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · App: Eat Next (iPhone)

Eat Next helps you find places to eat nearby, tag the ones you want to try or have visited, keep lists and learn your taste. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Summary

  • All of your data stays on your device.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no Eat Next server or backend.
  • There are no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • As configured for App Store nutrition labels: Data Not Collected.

Location

With your permission, Eat Next uses your location only while you are using the app to search for nearby places with Apple Maps (MapKit) and to show Apple's Look Around imagery. These searches are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Eat Next does not store your location history and does not send your location to us.

Restaurant websites

When you open a place, Eat Next may fetch that restaurant's public website to show its photos and a short summary. These requests go directly from your iPhone to that website; they do not pass through any server of ours. Like any web visit, the website can see a normal web request (for example your IP address). The summary is generated on your device with Apple Intelligence, and the page content is not sent to us or to any AI service.

Information stored on your device

Your tags lists, viewing history and the taste ranker that learns which places to show you first are all stored and computed on your iPhone. None of it is uploaded to us. Device backups follow your own iCloud or computer backup settings.

Sharing lists

You can share a list as a link or an image. Nothing is shared unless you choose to send it, and you choose who receives it and through which app. A shared link contains the places in that list so the recipient can open it.

Export, import and deleting your data

In Settings you can export your data to a file, import a file you exported earlier, or wipe all of the app's data from your device. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Permissions

  • Location (While Using the App) — to find nearby places with Apple Maps.

Children

Eat Next is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
