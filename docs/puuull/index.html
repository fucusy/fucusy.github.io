What it does

Hands-free counting that learns how you move.

Every set you save teaches Puuull how you move. Once you have saved 5 recorded sets, it compares its counting algorithms on your own sets and switches only when one is clearly more accurate. It all happens on your iPhone.

Good to know

Pull-up counting, explained.

Everything you need to get started with your iPhone pull-up counter.

How does Puuull count pull-ups?

Start a set and put your iPhone in a front pocket. Puuull uses the phone's motion sensors to count reps as you move, including when the screen is locked. You can correct the count before saving the set.

Do I need an Apple Watch to count pull-ups?

No. Your iPhone counts the reps. The optional Apple Watch companion displays stats sent from your iPhone; it does not count reps on its own.

Can I track my pull-up progress?

Yes. Puuull saves your sets and shows weekly, monthly, yearly and all-time totals, streaks and an activity grid. You can also add a set manually.

Does Puuull work with Apple Health?

With your permission, Puuull can save each recorded set as a Strength Training workout in Apple Health.

Is the pull-up counter free?

Yes. Puuull is free on the App Store, with no account and no ads. It requires an iPhone running iOS 26 or later.
