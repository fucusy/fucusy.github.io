Puuull

Pull-up counter for iPhone and Apple Watch

Count your pull-ups with your phone in your pocket. Tap Start, put the phone in your pocket, do your set and tap End. Fix the count if it's off, and Puuull learns how you move and counts more accurately over time.

  • Hands-free counting, even with the phone locked
  • History with a motion chart for every set
  • Stats and streaks, also on Apple Watch
  • Each recorded set saved to Apple Health as a workout
