Pull-up counter · iPhone & Apple Watch

Count your pull-ups, phone in pocket.

Tap Start, put your iPhone in a front pocket and do your set. Puuull counts reps from your phone's motion sensors, even when it's locked. Tap End, check the count, fix it if it's off, and the set is saved.

What it does

Hands-free counting that learns how you move.

Every set you save teaches Puuull how you move. Once you have saved 5 recorded sets, it compares its counting algorithms on your own sets and switches only when one is clearly more accurate. It all happens on your iPhone.

Privacy

No account, no sign-in, no ads.

Your sets are recorded and stored on your iPhone, and you can export or delete them at any time. Any data Puuull collects to improve counting is anonymous, not linked to you, never used for tracking and never sold. Apple Health data is never shared.
