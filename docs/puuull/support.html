App: Puuull (iPhone and Apple Watch) · Contact: qiang@hula.ai

Puuull counts your pull-ups from your iPhone's motion sensors while the phone sits in your pocket. No account or sign-in is needed.

How to use it

  1. Tap Start on the Live tab right before you get on the bar.
  2. Put the phone in a front trouser pocket. It's fine to lock it; Puuull keeps counting.
  3. Do your pull-ups. Reps are counted from the motion of your body as you pull up.
  4. Take the phone out and tap End. Check the count, fix it if it's off, then save.
  5. It learns from you. Every set you save teaches Puuull how you move. Fix the count when it's off, and counting gets more accurate over time, right on your iPhone.

FAQ

How accurate is the counting?

Puuull counts best when the phone is in a front trouser pocket and you stand still before and after the set. Walking to or from the bar while recording can add reps, so tap Start just before your set and End right after. After you have saved 5 recorded sets, Puuull compares its counting methods on your own sets and switches to one only when it is clearly more accurate for you. Correcting the count after a set helps it learn.

Does it work with Apple Watch?

In version 1.0 the Apple Watch app shows your stats (today, this week, your streak and your last session), synced from your iPhone. Counting is done on the iPhone.

Why does Puuull ask for Health access?

With your permission, Puuull saves each recorded set to Apple Health as a Strength Training workout. It does not read any Health data. You can change this any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Puuull. Puuull also asks for Motion & Fitness access, which it needs to count reps.

How do I export my data?

Open History, tap a set and choose Share recording data to send its sensor data as CSV files. All recordings are also visible in the Files app under On My iPhone › Puuull.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a single set from its detail screen in History, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device. Workouts already saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

Can I add a set I didn't record?

Yes. Use Add a set without recording on the Live tab, or the + button in History.
