Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · App: Puuull (iPhone and Apple Watch)

Puuull counts your pull-ups from your phone's motion while it sits in your pocket, and keeps a history and stats of your sets. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Summary

  • Everything Puuull records stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not track you.
  • As configured for App Store nutrition labels: Data Not Collected.

Information processed on your device

During a set, Puuull reads your iPhone's motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope) to count reps. It saves the motion recording, the rep count, the date and time and any note you type, on your iPhone only. The Lab view compares counting methods using these same recordings, also on your device.

If you use the Apple Watch app, your recent totals are sent from your iPhone to your watch over Apple's own device-to-device connection, so the watch can show your stats. This data does not pass through our servers.

Apple Health

With your permission, Puuull saves each recorded set to Apple Health as a Strength Training workout. Puuull does not read any data from Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising and is never shared with us or anyone else.

Sharing and export

Your data only leaves your device when you choose to: for example when you use Export All Data, share a set's recording data, or open the app's folder in the Files app. Where it goes is up to you. Device backups follow your own iCloud or computer backup settings.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect workout data, motion data, contact information, location, identifiers, or usage analytics. Puuull has no servers of its own and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Permissions

  • Motion & Fitness — to count your reps during a set.
  • Apple Health — to save each set as a workout.

Deleting your data

You can delete a single set from its detail screen, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Workouts already saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

Children

Puuull is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us in a way that conflicts with this policy, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
