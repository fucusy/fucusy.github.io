Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Last updated: October 31, 2026 · App: Puuull (iPhone and Apple Watch)

Puuull counts your pull-ups from your phone's motion while it sits in your pocket, and keeps a history and stats of your sets. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Summary

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Puuull may collect anonymous motion/sensor and workout session data to improve counting accuracy.
  • This data is not linked to your identity, is not used for tracking or advertising, and is not sold.
  • Apple Health data is never used for advertising and is not shared.

Information processed on your device

During a set, Puuull reads your iPhone's motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope) to count reps. It saves the motion recording, the rep count, the date and time and any note you type on your iPhone. Puuull also learns from the sets you save to count more accurately for you, and the Lab view compares counting methods using these same recordings.

If you use the Apple Watch app, your recent totals are sent from your iPhone to your watch over Apple's own device-to-device connection, so the watch can show your stats.

Information we may collect

To improve counting accuracy, Puuull may collect anonymous data such as:

  • Motion and sensor data recorded during a set;
  • Workout session data: rep counts (including counts you correct) and session timing;
  • Device information: device model and OS version;
  • Diagnostics: for example crash and performance information.

This data is not linked to your identity: we do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or advertising identifiers with it. It is used only to understand and improve how Puuull counts and works. It is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites, it is not used for advertising, and it is not sold.

Apple Health

With your permission, Puuull saves each recorded set to Apple Health as a Strength Training workout. Puuull does not read any data from Apple Health. Data from Apple Health (HealthKit) is never used for advertising and is not shared with anyone.

Sharing and export

You can export your own data at any time: for example by sharing a set's recording data or opening the app's folder in the Files app. Where it goes is up to you. Device backups follow your own iCloud or computer backup settings.

Permissions

  • Motion & Fitness — to count your reps during a set.
  • Apple Health — to save each set as a workout.

Deleting your data

You can delete a single set from its detail screen, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Workouts already saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app. Because any data we collect is anonymous, we cannot tie it back to you; if you have a question about it, contact us below.

Children

Puuull is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us in a way that conflicts with this policy, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The dates above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
