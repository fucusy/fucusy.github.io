Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026 · App: Diary Companion (日记伙伴)

Diary Companion helps you keep a handwritten journal and, after you capture a page, offers a calm on-device perspective. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Summary

  • Your diary pages and handwriting are processed on your device for recognition and reflection.
  • We do not require an account to use the core experience.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • As configured for App Store nutrition labels: Data Not Collected for developer data collection.

Information we process on your device

When you photograph or select a journal page, Diary Companion may access the camera or photo library with your permission. Handwriting recognition and reflection run locally on your device so you can read a private outside perspective without sending your life to our servers for that core flow.

Content you create (photos of pages, transcripts, and reflections) stays under your control on your device unless you deliberately share or export it using features provided by iOS or the app.

Information we do not collect

For the core Diary Companion experience described in our App Store privacy nutrition labels, we do not collect diary content, contact information, location, identifiers, or usage analytics for our own servers. We do not use your journal entries to train advertising models, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Permissions

  • Camera / Photos — to capture or import journal pages.
  • Other system permissions appear only if a future feature needs them, and only with your consent.

Children

Diary Companion is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us in a way that conflicts with this policy, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

隐私政策

生效日期：2026年9月22日 · 应用：日记伙伴（Diary Companion）

日记伙伴帮助你保留手写日记的习惯，并在拍下纸页后，在设备端提供温和的反思视角。 本政策说明应用如何处理相关信息。

摘要

  • 日记纸页与手写内容在设备端完成识别与反思。
  • 核心体验无需注册账户。
  • 我们不出售你的个人信息。
  • 按 App Store 隐私标签配置：不收集数据。

在设备端处理的信息

当你拍摄或选择日记页时，应用可能在你授权后使用相机或相册。手写识别与反思在本地运行， 以便你获得私密的第三方视角，而无需为了该核心流程把日记内容发送到我们的服务器。

我们不收集的信息

就 App Store 隐私营养标签所描述的核心体验而言，我们不会把日记内容、联系方式、位置、 标识符或使用分析收集到我们的服务器。我们不会用你的日记训练广告模型，也不会跨其他公司的 App 或网站追踪你。

权限

  • 相机 / 照片 — 用于拍摄或导入日记页。
  • 其他系统权限仅在未来功能确有需要、且经你同意后才会请求。
