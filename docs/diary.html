The science of the hand

Typing records quickly. Handwriting thinks in motion.

A keypress is a repeatable selection. A handwritten letter is a continuous path shaped by vision, touch and fine motor control. That richer sensorimotor loop is one reason the two modes feel—and register in the brain—differently.

The value of a second chair

Some patterns are easier to see from across the table.

Research on self-distancing suggests that considering an experience from an observer’s point of view can reduce emotional reactivity and repetitive thought. A trusted reader can help us step back. AI can make that kind of prompt available when another person is not.

“What might this look like if it happened to someone you care about?”
Useful, with honest boundaries

AI does not possess human care, can misunderstand you, and is not a therapist. Here its role is focused: offer a steady, nonjudgmental question; surface a possible pattern; help you look again.

What the research says—and what it does not.
